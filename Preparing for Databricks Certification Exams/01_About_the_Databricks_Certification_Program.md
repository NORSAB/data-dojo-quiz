# About the Databricks Certification Program

**Course:** Preparing for Databricks Certification Exams (Course 11 of 11)  
**Lesson:** 1 of 4  

---

Databricks Certification exams are a great way to validate your data and AI skills on the Databricks Platform. To get you started, we'll cover fundamental information about the program itself.

## Certification vs. Accreditation

Databricks offers two types of credentials you can earn to validate your skills and earn digital, shareable badges on social networks:

### 1. Certification
- **Format:** Proctored exam completed through the online examination portal, **Webassessor (Kryterion)**.
- **Requirements:** Stricter rules, identity verification, webcam/microphone monitoring, and secure browser lockdown.
- **Cost:** Associated registration fee (typically $200 USD).
- **Retake Policy:** Mandatory **14-day waiting period** between unsuccessful exam attempts.

### 2. Accreditation
- **Format:** Free, non-proctored quizzes completed directly through **Databricks Academy**.
- **Requirements:** Open-book learning checks with fewer rules.
- **Cost:** Free of charge.
- **Retake Policy:** Can be attempted as many times as needed with no waiting period.

---

## Certification Levels

Certification exams are offered at either an **Associate** or **Professional** level, depending on the material being assessed and where it falls in the Databricks learning pathways:

- **Associate Level:** Recommended for candidates with **~6+ months** of hands-on work experience with Databricks.
- **Professional Level:** Recommended for candidates with **~2+ years** of hands-on work experience with advanced Databricks platform capabilities.

> **Digital Badges:** Once you pass a certification, your digital verifiable badge is issued via **Credly / [credentials.databricks.com](http://credentials.databricks.com)** for sharing on LinkedIn, resumes, and portfolios.
