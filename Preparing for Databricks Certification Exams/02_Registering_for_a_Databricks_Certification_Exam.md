# Registering for a Databricks Certification Exam

**Course:** Preparing for Databricks Certification Exams (Course 11 of 11)  
**Lesson:** 2 of 4  

---

Databricks utilizes a third-party vendor (**Kryterion Webassessor**) to proctor and administer all certification exams worldwide.

## Key Account Considerations

### 1. Webassessor Test Taker Account
If you haven't already done so, you'll need to create a dedicated **Test Taker Account** on Webassessor. Your Webassessor account is completely separate from your company login or your Databricks Academy login. Always use a personal email address or ensure you keep access to the email tied to your credential history.

### 2. Forgotten Password Recovery
If you've forgotten your Webassessor password or username, use the self-service reset links on the Webassessor login screen. Do not create a duplicate Webassessor account, as this fragments your credential records and certification history.

### 3. Webassessor vs. Databricks Academy
Be aware that your **Test Taker Account on Webassessor** and your **Databricks Academy account** are two distinct systems:
- **Databricks Academy:** Delivers training courses, learning pathways, self-paced labs, and free accreditations.
- **Kryterion Webassessor:** Official proctoring platform that handles certification exam purchases, scheduling, proctored exam sessions, and score reporting.

## Step-by-Step Exam Registration Process

### Step 1: Register for Databricks Certification Exam

The following steps will guide you through how to create your Webassessor Test Taker account and register for proctored Databricks Certification Exams.

### Step 2: Checkout

On the next page, you can confirm you've selected the correct exam, date, and time before continuing to checkout. On the exam checkout page, you may enter a voucher code if you have one and the required payment information. Click Submit, then Check Out. Please note: Databricks will not issue retake vouchers for an exam.

**NOTE: The example here shows the Databricks Data Analyst Associate exam. PLEASE ENSURE THAT YOU REGISTER FOR THE CORRECT EXAM.**

### Step 3: Navigate to the Webassessor Website

Direct your browser to [https://webassessor.com/databricks](https://webassessor.com/databricks) (opens in a new tab). You will be presented with a log-in screen for taking Databricks Certification exams.

### Step 4: Select "Create New Account"

From the navigation menu at the top of the screen, click on "Create New Account" to begin the process.

### Step 5: Complete the Form

Complete all the required fields on the resulting form and click Save. The email address you use will be connected to any Databricks certifications and badges you earn, so be aware of if you'll have continued access to that email address in the future.

### Step 6: Login with Credentials

After logging in with correct credentials click on "Register for an Exam."

### Step 7: Select Exam

Select the desired Exam from the list shown by clicking on the "Register" button to the right. You'll be prompted to select virtual or in-person. Please note the fee to attempt each exam is  200 USD plus any applicable local taxes.

### Step 8: Choose Date and Time

On the next page, you will be prompted to select your preferred date and time for sitting the exam. An email reminder will be sent to the candidate two days before the scheduled exam date.

## Delivery Options: Online Proctored vs. Testing Center

- **Online Proctored:** Take the exam from your home or office computer using a webcam, microphone, and the Kryterion Sentinel lockdown software.
- **Onsite Testing Center:** Take the exam in person at an authorized Kryterion testing facility worldwide, where hardware and proctoring are provided on-site.
