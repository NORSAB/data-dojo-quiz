# Accessing Your Lab Environment

## How to launch your lab

- The lab environment is provided by Vocareum and opens in a new browser tab.
- It is preconfigured with the permissions and resources needed for the lab exercises.
- Third-party cookies must be enabled for the Vocareum experience to work correctly.

### Instructor-led training

From the session's **Content** area, select the LTI link to the lab environment.

### Self-paced training

From the course content navigation on the right, select the LTI link to launch the lab environment.

## Lab duration

- Each lab has a session timer displayed in the upper-right corner.
- When the timer expires, the lab ends and its resources are cleaned up.
- Selecting **End Lab** early also ends the lab and cleans up its resources.
- The total lab budget permits taking each lab three times.

## Wait for the resources

- Wait while the lab environment is configured.
- Provisioning can take 5–20 minutes depending on network latency.
- When ready, the Databricks workspace appears inside the Vocareum tab.

## Enable pop-ups from Databricks Academy

If Vocareum is blocked, the browser displays a blocked pop-up icon in the address bar.

In Chrome:

1. Open the three-dot menu and select **Settings**.
2. Under **Privacy and security**, select **Site settings**.
3. Under **Content**, select **Pop-ups and redirects**.
4. Under **Allowed to send pop-ups and use redirects**, select **Add**.
5. Add the Academy address appropriate to the account:
   - Customers: `https://customer-academy.databricks.com:443`
   - Partners: `https://partner-academy.databricks.com:443`
   - Employees: `https://employee-academy.databricks.com:443`

## Enable third-party cookies for Vocareum

1. Open browser settings and navigate to **Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data**.
2. Disable **Block third-party cookies**, or open `chrome://settings/cookies`.
3. To retain blocking for other sites, add `[*.]labs.vocareum.com` as an allowed exception.
