# What to Expect on Exam Day

**Course:** Preparing for Databricks Certification Exams (Course 11 of 11)  
**Lesson:** 3 of 4  

---

In addition to coming to your Databricks certification exam with applicable technical knowledge, ensuring you meet all hardware, software, and physical environment requirements is crucial for a smooth testing session.

## Environment & Hardware Requirements

### Computer Requirements

- **Personal PC Recommended:** Kryterion strongly recommends use of a non-work related or personal computer for online proctored (OLP) exams.
- **No Virtual Machines:** Virtual machines (VMs) are strictly prohibited and will be blocked by the lockdown software.
- **Secure Browser Installation:** You must download and install the official Kryterion Secure Browser (Sentinel). This requires Administrator privileges on your operating system. Launching the software must occur through Webassessor at exam time; refer to [How to Launch Online Proctored (OLP) Exam](https://kryterion.my.site.com/support/s/article/Launching-your-Online-exam).
- **Hardware Requirements:** Working webcam, microphone, and stable high-speed Internet; refer to [Online Testing Requirements](https://kryterion.my.site.com/support/s/article/Online-Testing-Requirements?language=en_US).
- **System Readiness Check:** Always run the [Kryterion System Readiness Check](https://www.kryterion.com/systemcheck/) well prior to exam day.

### Room & Workspace Requirements

- **Lighting & Privacy:** Choose a room that is well lit, quiet, and completely free from distractions and other people.
- **Government-Issued Photo ID:** Bring a valid government-issued ID. The name on the ID must match your Webassessor account name exactly.
- **Clutter-Free Desk:** Your immediate surroundings must be clutter-free: only one active computer, one active monitor (dual monitors strictly forbidden), one keyboard, and one mouse.
- **CCTV Cameras:** If your room has a security or CCTV camera, ensure your monitor screen is not exposed to it and the camera does not interfere with privacy.
- **No Cell Phones:** Mobile phones and smart devices are strictly prohibited in the testing area.

---

## Prohibited Behaviors & Exam Security Violations

You must follow strict proctoring behavioral rules throughout the entire examination. Engaging in prohibited behaviors can lead to immediate exam termination, score forfeiture, and potential disqualification from future Databricks credentials:

1. **Reading Exam Material Aloud:** Speaking questions or reading text aloud is prohibited; if audible to the proctor, your exam may be flagged or terminated.
2. **Unauthorized Test Aids:** Using books, notes, scrap paper, writing instruments, or phones not explicitly authorized by Databricks.
3. **Background Noise or Audio Disruption:** Excessive background noise or audio issues that interfere with proctoring. You must provide a quiet testing space.
4. **Loss of Video / Low Resolution:** Webcam disconnects, loss of camera feed, or poor lighting will pause or suspend your exam until video is restored.
5. **Leaving the Test Environment:** Leaving your seat or camera frame prior to exam completion is strictly forbidden and results in immediate forfeiture.
6. **Capturing Content / Recording:** Taking photos, screenshots, or pointing recording devices at exam questions is a major violation resulting in permanent ban.

> **Important Note:** Always follow the online proctor's chat instructions promptly and maintain focus on your screen throughout the entire test.
